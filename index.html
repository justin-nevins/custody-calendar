Custody Calendar

Enter the rotating schedule, the exchange times, the holidays and birthdays, and the page draws every month, lists every exchange with its time, flags any stretch over the day limit, and saves the result as a calendar file.

Schedule times are whole hours, local to wherever the exchange happens; press Build the calendar after any change

Calendar

The excess under the limit rule comes from the regular time next to the holiday: from the end of the stretch where it meets the other parent's next block; if the stretch ends with a holiday, from its start; if both ends are holidays, from the end of the regular time inside it. The cycle keeps its original dates.

Add to the schedule

Everything you add appears under "Your schedule" with its own settings and a Remove button. Add a regular schedule first (the long distance blocks, the same city two-week schedule, or both), then the holidays and birthdays that apply. A custom period takes any dates you type.

"Days before" and "days after" widen a period around its day. The start time applies on the first day and the end time on the last day; "end of day" means midnight at the end of that day. Specific dates replace the computed dates for the years you list, written as 2027-11-22 10:00 to 2027-11-29 10:00, several separated by semicolons. Order of priority when periods overlap: birthdays, Mother's Day and Father's Day; then Thanksgiving and Christmas; then vacation periods; then the regular schedule.
